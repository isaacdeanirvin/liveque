LiveQue business cards

Design proof at 2× actual size. Glen is deliverable 1, Isaac is deliverable 2. Two stock options shown, because the evidence and the aesthetics disagree.

White ink Gold foil Black ply Uncoated white ply
Why there are two options. White-on-black reads 45% slower in darkness (Dobres, Chahine & Reimer, Applied Ergonomics 2017). In bright light polarity makes no difference at all. In a dark room it dominates, and going bigger does not fix it. A load-in is exactly that room. Option A puts the drama on the front and the reading on a white back. Option B is the all-black card, which looks better in photos and worse in the hand.

Production spec

Trim 3.5 × 2 in. Document 3.75 × 2.25 in with 0.125 in bleed per edge. Safe area 3.25 × 1.75 in. Option A is duplex: black ply bonded to uncoated white, seam visible at the trim as a deliberate stripe. Option B is dyed-through black (Sirio Ultra Black or Colorplan Ebony), decorated with white screen ink and gold foil, no CMYK. Foil needs 0.5 pt minimum stroke and 8 pt minimum type. QR is 1.0 in minimum, vector, 4-module quiet zone, dark-on-light only.

Do not send this HTML to a printer. A browser export gives raster or badly structured vector, and a card is read at arm's length where that shows immediately. This is the design and the spec. The print file has to be rebuilt as real vector art with the foil on its own layer.
