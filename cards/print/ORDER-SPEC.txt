===========================================================================
STOCK AND FINISH
===========================================================================

Recommended:  18pt Colorplan Ebony, through-dyed, colour-matched edges
              White ink printing
              ~$87 CAD per 250, ~$174 CAD for 250 each of both brothers
              Jukebox promo code JUL20BC takes 20% off

Why through-dyed: the card is near-black on both faces. Printing black on
white stock shows a white edge on every cut and every scuff. Through-dyed
stock is black all the way through.

Foil was considered and rejected: foil cannot carry a scannable QR code
(ISO/IEC 18004 Annex K - specular reflection off polished metal interferes
with reading), and it cost about $836 CAD more across both brothers.

===========================================================================
IF THE PRINTER ASKS FOR VECTOR
===========================================================================

These are 600 DPI raster files. For business cards that is above the usual
300 DPI requirement and most printers, Jukebox included, accept it.

If your printer specifically demands vector (AI/EPS/vector PDF):
  - qr-getliveque.svg is a true vector QR, use that
  - the type would need outlining in Illustrator or Affinity
  - tell me and I will rebuild it as vector
