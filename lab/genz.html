THE RULES (EVERY SKIN)

Locked: teal request button, gold money elements, queue order logic, payment sheet, ON AIR badge, no autoplay ever, no follower counts, tonight-only stats. Gold is blocked in every skin's palette — money keeps its own color on all ten.
