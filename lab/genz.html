WHAT THE MUSICIAN CONTROLS

Wallpaper, accent color, banner, avatar ring, display font (6 curated), mood line (editable from stage), stars chips, sticker layer, anthem, top-8 song tiles, pinned 3. Bounded like Noplace — no raw CSS.

WHAT NEVER CHANGES

Teal request button, gold money elements, queue order logic, payment sheet, ON AIR badge, no autoplay, no follower counts, tonight-only stats. Gold is blocked in the performer's accent picker — money keeps its own color.

The lineage

Every section descends from a mechanic that already worked somewhere Gen Z lives.

From MySpace

  • Top 8 → Top Fans Tonight (resets nightly, winnable, monetized status)
  • Profile song → Anthem / Now Playing with live reactions
  • Comment wall → Shoutout Wall, tipped notes get gold borders
  • Glitter chaos → sticker layer + themes, bounded so it can't touch money
  • Mood → status line editable from the stage

From Gen-Z apps

  • Noplace: bounded color customization + "stars" interest chips
  • Discord: banner/accent/decoration flair layers
  • Airbuds: react-to-the-song as the social gesture
  • BeReal: night-scoped states, finite page, "you were here"
  • Receiptify/Wrapped: the tip receipt share card
