The first thing every player asks

No, they can't make you play Wonderwall.

Requests land in a list on your phone. You play what you want, skip what you don't, and delete anything you never want to see again. A tip moves a song up your list. It never puts a song on your stage.

How it works

One code on the table.

Free, or with a tip. Apple Pay, Google Pay, a card, or your Venmo.

4

The money is already yours

It goes to your own Stripe account and out to your bank on your normal schedule. We never hold it.

What it costs

We take nothing.
