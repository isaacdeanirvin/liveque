Welcome to LiveQue™
Four quick steps to your first gig.
  1. Add your songs. On Setup, import a Spotify playlist or add a few by hand.
  2. Set your tip amounts. On Setup, choose what fans can tap.
  3. Get your QR code. On Card, print it for your tables.
  4. Start your gig. On Live, your queue goes live for fans to request.
Getting paid
Fans can tip you — but to actually receive that money, you must connect your Stripe account. Payouts go straight to your bank. Until you connect it, fans can still request for free or tip through your Venmo/PayPal links. Takes about 2 minutes.

The Basics

Fans scan your QR code, browse your songs, and submit requests. You see them here in real-time. Tip requests rise to the top. Tap Play, then Complete when you're done.

Before Each Gig

Tap "Start New Gig" on the Dashboard. This clears your queue and resets cooldowns so you're starting fresh.

Live Queue

Quick Add gives you four random available songs — tap to add instantly. Shuffle for new options. Songs have a 2-hour cooldown after being played.

QR Code

Generate and download a print-ready card for your venue tables. 4×6 inches — fits standard photo holders.

Connection Status

Green means live. Yellow means reconnecting. Red means disconnected — refresh the page.
