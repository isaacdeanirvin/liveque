The Basics

Fans scan your QR code, browse your songs, and submit requests. You see them here in real-time. Tip requests rise to the top. Tap Play, then Complete when you're done.

Before Each Gig

Tap "Start New Gig" on the Dashboard. This clears your queue and resets cooldowns so you're starting fresh.
