How it works

One QR code on the table.

1

They scan it with their phone camera

They point their camera at the QR code, tap the link that pops up, and your song list opens right in their browser. Nothing to install, no account to make.

2

They pick a song

Free, or with a tip. Apple Pay, Google Pay, or a card.

3

It shows up on your phone

Tipped requests sit at the top with the amount on them. Silent, so nothing beeps at you mid song.

4

The money is already yours

It goes to your own Stripe account and out to your bank on your normal schedule. We take none of it.

What it costs

We take 0%.

No percentage, no monthly fee, no cut of your tips. Most request apps that call themselves free charge the person tipping you a service fee on top. We don't, so a $5 tip costs your crowd $5. The only money that comes out is what Stripe charges to process the card.

Someone tips you$5.00
Stripe card processing2.9% plus 30 cents−$0.45
What LiveQue takes$0.00
Lands in your bank$4.55

That flat 30 cents bites hardest on small tips. Two dollars lands as $1.64. Worth knowing before you set your amounts, and you should hear it here instead of finding it on a statement.

0% is our pricing today, not a promise about forever. If we ever introduce a fee you get 30 days notice first and you can leave before it starts. It is written into our Terms.

Your song list

They can only ask for songs you already play.

Originals and covers, side by side. Put your own songs on the list right next to the covers you play. The room that just heard you play one of your originals is the most likely to request it again, and buy you a drink for it.

Your list is the menu. Nobody can request something you never learned, so you are done saying “sorry, I don't know that one.” A tip moves a song up the list. It never puts a song on your stage.
